# Shared references

Bibliography for the Arasense research track. Add BibTeX keys as papers formalise.

## Core method
- **Izzaddin, A., et al. (2024).** *A new diagram for performance evaluation of
  complex models.* Stochastic Environmental Research and Risk Assessment (SERRA).
  — the Aras Diagram (the platform's scientific core).

## Skill scores and benchmarks
- Gupta, H. V., Kling, H., Yilmaz, K. K., & Martinez, G. F. (2009). *Decomposition
  of the mean squared error and NSE performance criteria.* J. Hydrology — KGE.
- Knoben, W. J. M., Freer, J. E., & Woods, R. A. (2019). *Technical note: Inherent
  benchmark or not? Comparing NSE and KGE scores.* HESS — the KGE = −0.41 benchmark.
- Taylor, K. E. (2001). *Summarizing multiple aspects of model performance in a
  single diagram.* JGR — the Taylor diagram (predecessor).

## Ensemble weighting and constrained projections
- Knutti, R., et al. (2017). *A climate model weighting scheme accounting for
  performance and interdependence.* GRL — ClimWIP foundations.
- Brunner, L., et al. (2020). *Reduced global warming from CMIP6 projections when
  weighting models by performance and independence.* Earth System Dynamics — ClimWIP.
- Sanderson, B. M., Knutti, R., & Caldwell, P. (2015). *A representative democracy
  to reduce interdependency in a multimodel ensemble.* J. Climate — independence.
- Tebaldi, C., & Knutti, R. (2007). *The use of the multimodel ensemble in
  probabilistic climate projections.* Phil. Trans. R. Soc. A.
- Giorgi, F., & Mearns, L. O. (2002). *Calculation of average, uncertainty range,
  and reliability of regional climate changes ... (REA method).* J. Climate.

## CMIP6, evaluation, data
- Eyring, V., et al. (2016). *Overview of the Coupled Model Intercomparison Project
  Phase 6 (CMIP6) experimental design and organization.* GMD.
- Eyring, V., et al. (2019). *Taking climate model evaluation to the next level.*
  Nature Climate Change — ESMValTool / systematic evaluation.
- IPCC AR6 WG1 (2021). *Climate Change 2021: The Physical Science Basis.*
- NASA GDDP-CMIP6 (Thrasher et al.) — downscaled daily CMIP6.
- Muñoz-Sabater, J., et al. (2021). *ERA5-Land: a state-of-the-art global reanalysis
  dataset for land applications.* ESSD.
