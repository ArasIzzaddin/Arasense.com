Method & honesty

How to read this atlas

Ensembles

Two depths of analysis

Atlas cities are screened with a 5-model trust ensemble. Bologna additionally has a full 34-model CMIP6 deep dive — which is why its two numbers (+10.8% screening, +14.2% full ensemble) differ. Both are shown, on purpose.

Data

What's underneath

ERA5-Land observations score each model's local skill via the Aras Diagram; trusted models are weighted into the projection. Baseline 1995–2014, future 2040–2059, scenario SSP2-4.5, 50 km radius per city.

Coverage

Cities across Europe, more with every pilot

Every ranked pin was computed with the same pipeline on the same settings — the ranking is comparable by construction. Bari / Puglia is the current pilot region; its evidence pack is in preparation.

Scope note. Atlas signals are screening-level evidence for prioritization and planning conversations — not a substitute for site-specific engineering studies. Screening ensembles are small by design; a full-ensemble deep dive is how a pin becomes decision evidence.
