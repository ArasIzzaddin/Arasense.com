Method & honesty

How to read this atlas

Ensembles

Two depths of analysis

Portfolio cities were screened with a 5-model trust ensemble. Bologna additionally has a full 34-model CMIP6 deep dive — which is why its two numbers (+10.8% screening, +14.2% full ensemble) differ. Both are shown, on purpose.

Data

What's underneath

ERA5-Land observations score each model's local skill via the Aras Diagram; trusted models are weighted into the projection. Baseline 1995–2014, future 2040–2059, scenario SSP2-4.5.

Coverage

Six pins today, more with every pilot

Each new engagement adds analyzed locations to the atlas. Bari / Puglia is the current pilot region — its full evidence pack is in preparation.

Scope note. Atlas signals are screening-level evidence for prioritization and planning conversations — not a substitute for site-specific engineering studies.
