Platform

What the platform is built to deliver

Arasense combines climate model evaluation, bias-aware interpretation, and flood-risk analytics into one technical stack. The objective is not another generic dashboard. The objective is a more defensible basis for planning, screening, and risk communication.

Climate Intelligence

Compare climate model behavior through structured signal decomposition instead of oversimplified ranking alone.

Flood Analytics

Use graph-based hydrological structure to approximate flood propagation faster for early-stage assessment and scenario review.

Decision Support

Support institutions, infrastructure owners, planners, and advisory teams with clearer evidence for adaptation and risk decisions.
