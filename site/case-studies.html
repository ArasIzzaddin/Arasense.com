Case 01

Bologna · flood-driving rainfall

METRIC · MAX 1-DAY PRECIPITATION (RX1DAY) BASELINE · 1995–2014 FUTURE · 2040–2059 SCENARIO · SSP2-4.5

All 34 CMIP6 models were scored against observed climate for the Bologna region before any projection was made. 32 passed skill screening; the projection uses only those, weighted by measured skill.

Case 02

Italian flood-exposure portfolio

The same trust-first workflow applied across five cities, ranked by worsening flood-driving rainfall signal. This is how asset owners, consultants, and risk teams compare exposures with one consistent method.

RankLocationToday2050ChangeAgreement
1Rome39.91 mm47.40 mm+7.49 mm (+19%)100%
2Milan63.90 mm72.10 mm+8.20 mm (+13%)79%
3Florence46.17 mm51.45 mm+5.28 mm (+11%)80%
4Bologna44.20 mm48.95 mm+4.75 mm (+11%)100%
5Venice52.96 mm54.70 mm+1.74 mm (+3%)56%

Note how the agreement column changes the story: Venice's +3% carries only 56% model agreement, a signal to treat with caution, and exactly the kind of nuance a single ranking score would bury.
